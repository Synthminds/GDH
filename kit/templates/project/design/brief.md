# Design brief

Status: template

The `align` skill collects the answers. The `design-brief` skill writes this file and keeps its format.
The status line above is the authority. It reads `Status: template`, `Status: draft`, or `Status: confirmed YYYY-MM-DD by <who>`.
The front matter `status` repeats its first word, so that other readers can tell a template from a filled brief.
Mark a line `(proposed)` while it waits for the user. Mark a line `ASSUMED` when the user skipped it or said "you decide".

## 1. Subject

What is being designed, in one sentence. What it is not, in one sentence.

## 2. People and context

- User: one person, with two specific details.
- Also touched by: buyer, approver, operator, bystander.
- Not for: who this is not designed for.
- Context: device, place, time pressure, state of mind, connection, languages. What happens just before and just after.
- Evidence: for each claim, observed, reported or assumed.
